Penang Pulse

How evenly are clinics, schools and transit stops spread across Penang?

In progress

Short answer

Most residents of Timur Laut live within a kilometre of a public clinic. In Seberang Perai Selatan, about 1 in 5 live more than 3 km away, and the gap is widest for clinics rather than schools.

Method

  1. Collect public clinic, school and transit stop points from the PeGIS layers.
  2. Join each mukim to its Census 2020 population.
  3. Compute road network distance from each population-weighted mukim centroid to the nearest facility of each type.
  4. Band the distances into under 1 km, 1 to 3 km and over 3 km, and sum residents per band and district.
  5. Bootstrap mukims within each district to get 95% intervals.

Limitations

  • Mukim centroids hide variation within each mukim, so people at the edges may be further away than shown.
  • Private clinics are excluded, so the gap overstates distance to any clinic.
  • OpenStreetMap road coverage is thinner in rural Seberang Perai, which can stretch distances there.
  • The 2020 census population is six years old.
