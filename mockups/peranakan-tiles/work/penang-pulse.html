Penang Pulse

How evenly are clinics, schools and transit stops spread across Penang?

The answer

Most residents of Timur Laut live within a kilometre of a public clinic. In Seberang Perai Selatan, about 1 in 5 live more than 3 km away, and the gap is widest for clinics rather than schools.

Key numbers

of Seberang Perai Selatan residents live over 3 km from a public clinic.

The evidence

Distance to the nearest clinic, by district

Each tile is a district. Its bands fill from the floor up: jade for residents under 1 km from a public clinic, mustard for 1 to 3 km, rose for over 3 km. Pick a tile to see its numbers and find it on the map.

SP is Seberang Perai. Shares are of residents, by road distance from population-weighted mukim centroids.

How it was made

Method

  1. Collect facility points for clinics, schools and transit stops from PeGIS.
  2. Join each mukim to its 2020 census population.
  3. Compute road network distance from each population-weighted mukim centroid to the nearest facility.
  4. Band distances into under 1 km, 1 to 3 km and over 3 km, and sum residents per band.
  5. Bootstrap mukims to get intervals for each district share.

Limitations

  • Centroids hide within-mukim variation, so some residents are closer or further than their band says.
  • Private clinics are excluded, which overstates the distance to any clinic.
  • OpenStreetMap road coverage is thinner in rural Seberang Perai.
  • The 2020 census population is six years old.
