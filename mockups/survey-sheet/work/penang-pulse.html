Penang Pulse

In progress

How evenly are clinics, schools and transit stops spread across Penang?

Most residents of Timur Laut live within a kilometre of a public clinic. In Seberang Perai Selatan, about 1 in 5 live more than 3 km away, and the gap is widest for clinics rather than schools.

Access by district

Pick a district to read its numbers in the margin, or click it on the map. Use the legend to show or hide each kind of service.

Method

  1. Collect public clinic, school and transit stop points from PeGIS.
  2. Join each mukim to its Census 2020 population.
  3. Compute road network distance from each population-weighted mukim centroid to the nearest facility.
  4. Band the distances into under 1 km, 1 to 3 km and over 3 km.
  5. Bootstrap mukims to get 95% intervals for each district share.

Limitations

  • Centroids hide variation inside each mukim.
  • Private clinics are excluded, so access looks worse than it is for people who can pay.
  • OpenStreetMap road coverage is thinner in rural Seberang Perai.
  • The 2020 census population is six years old.
